Telemetry Design

  • ✓ UI/UX | Interface
  • ✓ System Design | Figma

Collaborated with a multidisciplinary team to design the telemetry interface of a spacesuit monitoring system, focused on tracking and displaying the bio data of astronauts in real time. The interface was designed to surface critical health metrics — including heart rate, oxygen levels, and suit pressure — in a clear, high-legibility format suited for high-stress environments. We developed a cohesive system architecture in Figma, balancing information density with intuitive navigation to support astronaut safety and mission efficiency. The project came in as a finalist in the NASA Spacesuit User Interface Technologies (SUIT) Challenge.

EV

Designed the EV (Extravehicular) display, the astronaut's primary interface during moonwalks — surfacing suit status, navigation, and mission timers at a glance.

PLSS

Designed the PLSS (Portable Life Support System) interface, tracking oxygen levels, CO2 scrubbing, cooling, and battery life to keep astronauts safe outside the spacecraft.

LTV

Designed the LTV (Lunar Terrain Vehicle) interface, giving astronauts real-time navigation, hazard alerts, and vehicle diagnostics while operating the rover on the lunar surface.
