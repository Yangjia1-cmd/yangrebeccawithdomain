Bio Wars

  • • Ideation | Strategy
  • • Graphic Design | Illustrator
  • • Board Game | Card Design
  • • Packaging

Bio Wars is a spatial strategy game that mimics plant competition and uses light to grow.

The goal:

  1. Achieve blooming by growing.
  2. Cast shadows onto rivals and obstruct their growth.

Drawing from the blueprints of the natural world, every block functions as a specialized cellular organelle. From the dense, impact-absorbing lattice of the honeycomb to the seamless movement of the starfish, these traits grant your organism unique biological advantages. Adapt your structure to the environment, trigger symbiotic boosts, and unlock dormant traits to survive the harsh ecosystem.

Playtesting

Ideation

  • Mapping specialized evolution strategies onto blocks.
  • Inspired by biomimicry products and biology from my Are.na channel — how can we map that into a game?
  • Mimicking plant competition and survival strategies.
  • Developing spatial thinking with the elements of light and shadow.

Prototype

I made a low-fidelity prototype out of cardstock paper, tape, and glue. I then user tested it several times with friends before finalizing the rules of the game.
